# Privacy Policy — Timer

*Last updated: 2026-11-15*

**English**
Timer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline. Your settings (duration, sound, language) and your activity summary (active time and breaks) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, and are removed when you uninstall the app. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. The app shows a link to nextquo.com; it opens in your browser only if you click it, and the app itself sends no data. Contact: gab.carpediem@gmail.com
