# Privacy Policy — Timer

*Last updated: 2026-11-23*

**English**
Timer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline. Your settings (duration, sound, language, and the optional sound file you choose) and your activity summary (active time and breaks) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, and are removed when you uninstall the app. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. The app shows a link to nextquo.com and a link to an optional voluntary-donation page (ko-fi.com/gabrielem); they open in your browser only if you click them, and the app itself sends no data. The optional inactivity check only reads how long it has been since the last keyboard or mouse input (never which keys you press or what you type); the value is used on your device and is not stored or sent anywhere. If you choose to export a CSV file or save a backup, the file is written only where you choose on your device; the app does not send it anywhere. Contact: gab.carpediem@gmail.com
